About me

I'm a senior at Penn State's Smeal College of Business, majoring in Finance with a minor in Supply Chain and Information Systems. I graduate in May 2027.

I'm from Singapore, though I moved around a lot growing up. Before university I served in the Republic of Singapore Navy as a naval warfare systems specialist. I spent my first two years at Penn State Berks, where I led the International Club and mentored new international students, and moved to University Park in Fall 2025.

This summer I interned in corporate banking at Citi in Singapore, and that's where information systems started to matter to me. Getting a credit approval through took ages, and the analysis itself was rarely the slow part. The information sat in three systems that didn't connect, so people spent most of their time chasing it down. IST 110 is my way into the systems side of problems like that.

Outside class I take photos and go to the gym.

About this site

The site is plain HTML and CSS, with no website builder, and it's hosted on GitHub Pages. I picked that over a builder because making it uses what the course teaches, from HTML in Lesson 9 to version control, and because it's something I can keep adding to after the semester ends. My Milestone 1 plan explains the choice in more detail.

How I used AI

I used Claude, an AI assistant, to build this site. It wrote the HTML and CSS from the plan in my Milestone 1 document, set up the layout, and drafted the short task descriptions and summaries from my submitted files. For the reflections, I answered questions about each piece of work, and Claude turned my answers into finished paragraphs, filling in details from my submitted work. I chose what went on the site and checked every page.
