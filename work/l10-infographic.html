L10 Security infographic: the WannaCry attack

Lesson 10, Security. Assignment, submitted 8 November 2026.

The task

Pick a security concept from the zyBook, research it and design an infographic that explains it. AI-generated designs weren't allowed.

What I made

A one-page infographic on the 2017 WannaCry ransomware attack, told in six steps: what ransomware is, the skipped update that let it in, how it spread without anyone clicking, the ransom demand, the kill switch that stopped it, and how to protect yourself. It locked more than 300,000 computers in over 100 countries within days.
