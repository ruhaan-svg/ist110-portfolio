L02 Binary representation

Lesson 2, History and Basics. Assignment, submitted 10 September 2026.

The task

Practise converting between decimal and binary with tutorials and games, then write up what binary is, why it matters and how the practice went.

What I made

A two-page write-up. My worked example converts 156 to binary by taking away the largest power of two that fits, over and over: 156 is 128 + 16 + 8 + 4.

156 in binary is 10011100
Place value1286432168421
Bit10011100

The idea I took away is that binary carries no meaning on its own. The same byte, 01000001, is the number 65 or the letter A depending on the standard used to read it.
