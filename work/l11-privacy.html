L11 What do companies know about me?

Lesson 11, Privacy. Assignment, submitted 15 November 2026.

The task

Review what I give away on social media and what my Google account stores, search for myself on Google and on two people-search sites, and find out how listings can be removed.

What I made

A three-page review. My Google account had Web & App Activity switched on with auto-delete off, YouTube history on, and more than 39 outside apps connected through Sign in with Google. My full name is uncommon, so searching it brought up almost nothing but me. Whitepages and Spokeo had no record at all, which makes sense: they build profiles from US public records, and as an international student I have very few.
