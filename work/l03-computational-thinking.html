L03 Computational thinking infographic

Lesson 3, Computing Concepts. Assignment, submitted 19 September 2026.

The task

Design an infographic in Adobe Express explaining four problem-solving ideas: decomposition, pattern recognition, abstraction and algorithms. Generative AI wasn't allowed.

What I made

An infographic called Four Ways to Think Like a Computer. It explains all four ideas through one everyday problem, making lunch for five friends: break the job into smaller jobs, notice every sandwich has the same layers, ignore details like the bread type, and write the steps as a recipe that repeats until five are made. The last panel shows the same process in a GPS app planning a route.

Download the original (PNG image, 475 KB)

Reflection

Using one example, making lunch for five friends, let me show the four ideas as one process instead of four separate definitions. Decomposition, pattern recognition, abstraction and algorithms only really make sense when you see them working together, and a sandwich order is something everyone gets.

AI wasn't allowed on this one, so I kept the design simple. Looking at it now, I'd cut a lot of the text and let the visuals do more of the explaining.
